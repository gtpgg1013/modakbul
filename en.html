A quiet campfire for one. Write down what has been weighing on you. The sheet you wrote on settles over the logs, bends around the wood, and slowly burns away. Keep good memories as polaroids by the fire.

No accounts or ads. Your writing stays on your device, and burned notes are not kept. The app works offline and checks public store information for updates.
